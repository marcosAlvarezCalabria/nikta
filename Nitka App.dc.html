Meet Natalia

The heart behind NITKA.

With nearly 30 years of sewing experience, Natalia has dedicated her life to creating and restoring garments with care and precision.

Before moving to Ireland, she ran her own tailoring business in Ukraine, where she specialised in creating coats, sheepskin coats and leather jackets from scratch. Every piece was individually crafted, from the first pattern to the final stitch.

After relocating to Ireland, she spent the last two years working as a professional seamstress in a local alterations shop, gaining valuable experience with Irish customers and their needs.

Today, all of that knowledge and experience comes together at NITKA.

Whether it’s a simple repair, a perfect alteration, or giving your favourite garment a second life, every item is treated with the same attention to detail and craftsmanship.

Thank you for trusting us with the clothes you love.
